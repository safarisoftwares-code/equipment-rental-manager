It applies to all users of the Service, wherever located, and complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The Data Controller responsible for your personal data is:

2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Email address
  • Password (stored only as a bcrypt hash — never in plain text)
  • Name (optional)
  • Company name, phone, email, address, and Tax PIN (if provided by you)

2.2 Client & Rental Data You Enter

  • Client names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, Tax PINs
  • Rental item descriptions, dates, costs, quantities, amounts paid, tax amounts
  • Free-text notes you add to a client record

2.3 Technical Data (automatic)

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and access timestamps — collected by our hosting provider (Render) for security and uptime purposes

We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. We do not run any third-party analytics scripts.

3. How We Use Your Data & Legal Basis

  • To provide the Service — creating your account, storing your client and rental records, generating invoices and reports. Legal basis: performance of contract.
  • To secure the Service — authentication, fraud prevention, abuse detection. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • To comply with law — tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations (e.g., Kenya Revenue Authority). Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To communicate essential notices — service outages, security alerts, material changes. Legal basis: legitimate interest / contract.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your data for marketing unless you explicitly opt in.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share data only with the following service providers ("Processors") strictly necessary to run the Service:

  • Render Services, Inc. (hosting + PostgreSQL database) — servers located in the United States and European Union
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (CDN for Font Awesome)
  • SheetJS (CDN for spreadsheet export library)
  • jsPDF (CDN for PDF generation library)

Each Processor is contractually bound to protect data and use it only for the agreed purpose. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or a lawful request from a Kenyan or foreign authority with jurisdiction.

5. International Data Transfers

Because our hosting provider (Render) stores data in the United States and European Union, your data will be transferred outside Kenya. We rely on:

  • Adequacy decisions or contractual safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) where required
  • Your consent as a user of the Service, per Section 48 of the Kenya DPA

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active, and for 30 days after deletion (backup window)
  • Client & rental records: retained for as long as you keep them in the Service. When you delete a client, its records are removed immediately
  • Financial / tax records: retained for 7 years to comply with the Kenya Tax Procedures Act
  • Logs: retained by Render for up to 30 days

7. Data Security

We implement the following measures:

  • All passwords hashed with bcrypt (industry-standard)
  • All traffic over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Database connections encrypted (SSL enforced)
  • JWT-based session tokens with 7-day expiry
  • Access controls on the database limited to the application
  • Automatic backups by Render

Breach notification: In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) within 72 hours and affected users without undue delay, as required by Section 43 of the Kenya DPA.

8. Your Rights

Under the Kenya DPA (and GDPR where applicable), you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (you can export from the Service at any time)
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya: www.odpc.go.ke

To exercise any right, email info@safarisoftwares.co.ke. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Data

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us immediately.

10. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. The only client-side storage we use is your browser's localStorage to keep you logged in (the JWT token). You can clear this at any time by logging out.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced inside the Service at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
